Proposals Seed Finder

Pick up to three proposals you want in the first batch, and the finder lists world seeds that open with them. Seeds are case-sensitive: KWZ95 and kwz95 are different worlds. Pick the game version and scenario first: each scenario deals from its own proposal pack.

Day-0 proposals

Seedrng_seedFirst batch

Check a seed

How it works: the game hashes the seed text (Godot String.hash()) into rng_seed, seeds Godot's PCG32 generator with it, shuffles the proposals eligible on day 0 once, and offers the first three. Proposal weights don't affect this first batch. Eligible on day 0 means: not locked, no prerequisite proposal, unlock day 0, and no "Requires …" deployment condition.
